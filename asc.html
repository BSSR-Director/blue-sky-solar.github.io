IEF AMERICAN SOLAR CHALLENGE
Testing endurance through 1,500 miles (2,414 k) from Minnesota to Texas.
Our team is now working on developing a car to appear for the 2028 American Solar Challenge (ASC). Our team is testing the boundaries of engineering and energy efficiency as we research and design to produce our Generation 13 vehicle. We will first be competing in the Formula Sun Grand Prix (FSGP), the official qualifier for the ASC road race. At the race, our team will be pushing the boundaries of solar vehicle design as we traverse thousands of kilometers across the American terrain.
